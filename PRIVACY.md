# Privacy Policy – Elad Running Coach

Last updated: 30 September 2026

Elad Running Coach is a private personal running-coach application used through WhatsApp.

## Data processed

The service may process WhatsApp messages, phone number identifiers, running and training information supplied by the user, and technical logs needed to operate and troubleshoot the service.

## Purpose

The data is used only to provide personalized running-coach responses, retrieve or update training information through connected services when authorized, maintain conversation context, and operate the service.

## Sharing

Data may be processed by service providers required to operate the application, including Meta/WhatsApp, Render, Google Gemini, GitHub, and Tredict, subject to their respective terms and privacy policies. The application does not sell personal data.

## Retention

Conversation and application state may be retained as needed to provide continuity. Technical logs may be retained for troubleshooting and security.

## Deletion

The user can request deletion of stored application data by sending the Hebrew word **מחק** or the command **/delete** to the coach in WhatsApp.

See also: [Data Deletion Instructions](./DATA_DELETION.md)

## Contact

For privacy questions, contact the operator of Elad Running Coach.
